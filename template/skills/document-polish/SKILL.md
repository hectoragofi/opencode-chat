## What I do

- Tighten wording, fix grammar, and make structure scannable (headings, lists, tables).
- Match a requested tone (formal, friendly, executive-summary) without changing meaning.
- Flag missing information instead of inventing it.

## When to use me

Use this when the user asks to improve, rewrite, proofread or format a piece of writing, or says the output "should look professional".

## How to do it

1. Read the draft fully before changing anything.
2. Keep facts, numbers and names exactly as written — only improve expression.
3. Prefer short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and tables for comparisons.
4. End with a one-line summary of what changed.
